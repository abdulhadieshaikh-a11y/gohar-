# Photos needed from the client

_Updated 27 Oct 2026. Every official image on gohargoc.com is already on the site. More pictures must come from the client._

## How to send
Put the photos in the matching folder inside **`incoming-photos/`** in the project, for example `incoming-photos/gohar-complex-shopping-mall/`. Website Manager then crops, optimises, names, describes and places them, with no repeats across pages.

**Quality rules (important for a sharp website):**
- Original files straight from the camera, the photographer or the architect. **Not WhatsApp copies** and not screenshots, because WhatsApp shrinks and blurs images.
- At least **2500 px wide** (any modern phone or camera photo is fine).
- No text, logos, captions or watermarks burned into the picture.
- Say for each one whether it is a **real photo** or a **3D render**. The site labels them honestly.
